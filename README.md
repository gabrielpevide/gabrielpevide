## 📱 Apps shipped on the App Store

### 🕵️ [Mimic](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mimic-follow-smart-money/id6757661100) — *Follow the smart money*
Real-time tracking of insider and congressional stock trades. Aggregates 7,900+ trades from corporate executives and politicians across 40+ major companies — SEC filings and congressional disclosures in one clean feed.
- Live insider & congressional trades, daily
- Watchlists with push alerts for specific names
- Market Pulse sentiment dashboard
- Insider profiles with holdings & trade history

🔗 [mimicapp.app](https://www.mimicapp.app)

### 🛂 [VisaWatch](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/visawatch-uscis-case-tracker/id6767952941) — *Never miss a USCIS update*
Monitors USCIS immigration cases 24/7 and sends instant push notifications when status changes — usually before the official email arrives. My flagship app.
- Instant push, often before USCIS emails
- Complete case history with timestamps
- Visa Bulletin tracking & priority-date alerts
- Unlimited family cases, one price, zero ads

🔗 [visawatch.app](https://www.visawatch.app)

### 🎓 [Certly](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/certly-ai-cert-exam-prep/id6760678168) — *Study smart. Get certified.*
Exam prep for the Claude Certified Architect — Foundations certification. Scenario-based questions, a hands-on Prompt Lab graded instantly offline, and full exam simulations.
- 151 scenario-based practice questions
- Prompt Lab — prompt drills graded instantly, offline
- Full exam simulations, scored 100–1000
- Spaced repetition, daily challenges & streaks

🔗 [certlyexam.app](https://certlyexam.app)

### 🎖️ [Tagmuli](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tagmuli-miluim-pay-rights/id6782723834) — *Every reserve-duty benefit, in one place*
Calculates exactly what Israeli military reservists (miluim) are owed — from National Insurance, the Tax Authority, the military, and employers — and walks them through claiming each one. Fully on-device, no government login, no server.
- Service journal — automatic reserve-duty day counting
- Rights engine — benefits, tax credits & commander bonuses
- Self-employed business-rehabilitation grant calculator
- Claims center with per-portal deadline alerts

🔗 [tagmuli-website.vercel.app](https://tagmuli-website.vercel.app)

### 📸 [Assignment](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/assignment-street-photo-nyc/id6796157494) — *Know what's worth shooting today*
Reads New York's official event permits every morning and ranks the day's parades, protests, festivals and shoots by editorial priority — a shot list for street photographers, not a feed. Built out of my own photography work.
- Daily assignments ranked by editorial priority
- Sunrise, sunset & golden-hour times per location
- Real distance, maps & street closures, five boroughs
- Coverage log and Instagram share cards

🔗 [assignment-app-beta.vercel.app](https://assignment-app-beta.vercel.app)

### 🩺 [Mileage](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mileage-body-age-test/id6793303499) — *Your body has an odometer now*
Reads four systems with the iPhone's own sensors and Apple Health — cardio, neural, motor and recovery — and turns them into a single body-age number in about 60 seconds. Fully on-device, nothing uploaded.
- One body-age number in about 60 seconds
- Cardio, neural, motor & recovery, read system by system
- Trend line as you retest, plus a weekly maintenance plan
- No account, nothing leaves the phone

🔗 [mileage-app-rust.vercel.app](https://mileage-app-rust.vercel.app)

### 🗽 [CitizenReady](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/citizenready-civics-test-prep/id6798065737) — *Walk into the interview ready*
Every question on the official U.S. naturalization civics test — all 128 of the 2025 version and all 100 of the 2008 one — with accepted answers, plain-English explanations, and mock exams that follow the real interview format.
- The complete official bank, 2025 and 2008 tests
- Eight themed worlds broken into short lessons
- Four mock modes, including the realistic interview run
- Smart Review, 65/20 mode and a readiness score

🔗 [citizen-ready-rust.vercel.app](https://citizen-ready-rust.vercel.app)
